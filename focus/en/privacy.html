Privacy policy · 5 September 2026

Your attention is yours.
So are your settings.

This policy describes Focus for iPhone and iPad and the separately distributed native Mac app. The developer is Adir Ben Ami. Contact us at adir121@protonmail.com.

iPhone and iPad

Focus stores your daily schedule, manually entered website addresses, Apple Screen Time selections, and language preferences on your device. The app and its local extensions share this information through an Apple App Group to apply your blocking plan. A small local preference remembers the last encouragement shown.

Focus has no user accounts, advertising, tracking SDKs, or usage analytics. It does not upload your selections, schedule, or browsing history to a developer-operated server. The encouragement is included in the app; no AI service is contacted during use.

Permissions and Apple services

Focus requests Screen Time authorization to apply the restrictions you choose. Apple controls authorization, system enforcement, device protection, and device backups. Focus does not implement cross-device synchronization. Your device settings and Apple's services may include app data in backups. Local operating-system diagnostics may contain system error details; Focus does not automatically upload those logs.

The native Mac app

The current Mac app uses a local proxy to forward permitted browser traffic to the destinations you request. For HTTP, the proxy handles the request locally. For HTTPS, it forwards encrypted traffic without decrypting it or installing a certificate. Focus does not keep a persistent browsing history. Blocking settings, the original network settings needed for restoration, and language preferences are stored locally. Local connection diagnostics can include a network-service name. Websites, DNS services, and Apple services follow their own privacy policies.

Purchases and support

App Store purchases are processed by Apple. Focus does not receive your payment-card information. If you choose to email support, the support inbox receives your email address and the content and attachments you send. They are used to respond to your request and handle related issues. Email is handled by the email providers involved, including Proton Mail for the support inbox. Avoid sending passwords, full browsing histories, or unnecessary personal information. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the address above; applicable legal retention obligations may still apply.

Your choices

You can edit saved blocking settings outside the active blocking window and add websites during it. You can revoke Screen Time authorization in device Settings. Removing the iPhone or iPad app removes its local app data according to Apple's system behavior; backup copies are managed separately through Apple. On Mac, restore the network settings from Focus before removing the app. Removing the Mac application bundle alone does not necessarily remove its Application Support files or restore proxy settings.

This support website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add cookies, advertising, or analytics to this website. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This applies when you visit these web pages and is separate from the app’s local settings. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Changes

If Focus's data practices change, this policy will be updated. The date above identifies this version.

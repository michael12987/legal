How do I start?

On iPhone or iPad, allow Screen Time access, select websites and daily hours, then save. In the Mac App Store version, choose websites and hours, enable daily blocking and save; approve Focus in System Settings if macOS asks. The same window repeats every day.

Can I add a website during a block?

Yes. Additions are saved for future days as well. Removing websites, changing hours, and turning the plan off inside Focus become available after the block ends.

Why are some blocked screens different?

Some blocking screens are provided by your device’s system. Encouragement is available on supported Focus screens.

Does closing the app stop blocking?

On iPhone and iPad, the saved plan continues while Screen Time permission remains active. In the Mac App Store version, the saved schedule continues even after you quit Focus, while its system permission remains enabled. Encouragement appears while Focus is open.

My connection needs checking

Open Focus and check its status. Confirm you are using the latest version. If an unselected website still cannot load, contact support. In an earlier Mac version with a Restore Network Settings option, use that option before removing the app.

Why can a selected website still appear?

On Mac, content already loaded or a connection already open may remain available. Encrypted DNS, a VPN or direct IP access may bypass blocking. Check the saved schedule and permissions in Focus. The daily count reflects blocked requests and may differ from the number of times you tried to visit a site.
